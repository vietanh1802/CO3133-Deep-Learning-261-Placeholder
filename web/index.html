Deep Learning
Course Project

This site documents the course project of Group Placeholder for CO3133. Assignment 1 studies image classification on MNIST, Fashion-MNIST and CIFAR-10, Assignment 2 studies semantic segmentation of underwater images on SUIM, and Assignment 3 covers multimodal learning. Each assignment page reports the problem and data, the methods, the experimental setup, the results and an error analysis, with links to the code, reports and checkpoints.

01 Classification

Image
classification

Compares linear, MLP, CNN, GRU, LSTM and Transformer classifiers. Fashion-MNIST is the main dataset; MNIST was used during development and CIFAR-10 extends the comparison to colour images.

02 Segmentation

Semantic segmentation
of underwater images

Assigns one of eight classes to every pixel of SUIM images. A U-Net trained from scratch is compared with a pretrained DeepLabV3, followed by a controlled experiment on the loss function.
