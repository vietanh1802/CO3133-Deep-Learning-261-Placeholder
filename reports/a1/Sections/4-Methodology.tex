$z=Wx_{\rm flat}+b\in\mathbb{R}^{10}$. Optimize cross-entropy from logits;
do not apply softmax before \texttt{CrossEntropyLoss}.

\subsection{Loss, prediction, and evaluation metrics}
\label{sec:metrics}
For logits $z_i$ and target $y_i$, use
\[
 \mathcal{L}_{\rm CE}=-\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N
 \log\frac{\exp(z_{i,y_i})}{\sum_{k=0}^9\exp(z_{i,k})}.
\]
Report accuracy and macro-F1, where macro-F1 averages the per-class F1 scores
equally over all ten classes. State the precision/recall definition and the
zero-division policy used by the implementation.
